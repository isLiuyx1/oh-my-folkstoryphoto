## Humanizer-zh

This project adapts general human-writing review ideas from
[Humanizer-zh](https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh), including removing filler,
breaking formulaic structure, varying rhythm, trusting the reader, and avoiding
manufactured quotable conclusions.

Humanizer-zh is copyright its respective contributors and is distributed under
the MIT License. The upstream license is available at:
https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh/blob/main/LICENSE

No upstream source code is bundled. The rules in
`skills/oh-my-folkstoryphoto/references/human-writing.md` are an original
adaptation for found-footage stories and per-image captions.
